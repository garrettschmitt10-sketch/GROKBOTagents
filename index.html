Two crystal keeps face across a river of starlight. Spend Aether, drop your court, and shatter the rival Crownspire before the sky-lane closes.

Bot mind

How to play

  • You command Aurora Keep against the Dusk Court on a vertical duskglass arena.
  • Aether regenerates over time (max 10). Cards cost Aether to deploy.
  • Hold 4 cards. Play one and the next from your 8-card deck slides in.
  • Tap a card, then tap your half of the arena — or drag the card onto the field.
  • Spells can be aimed anywhere. Troops and wards must land on legal ground.
  • Destroy the enemy Crownspire to win, or lead in lanterns when time expires.
  • Keys 1–4 select cards. Esc cancels. Same Aether rules for you and the bot.
