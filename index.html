Riverwatch · Dustfront

IRON RIVER

Riverwatch and Dustfront duel across a flooded crossing. Spend Supply, drop your squad, and take the rival Strongpoint before the clock runs out.

Opposition

How to play

  • You command Riverwatch against Dustfront on a vertical river-crossing arena.
  • Supply regenerates over time (max 10). Cards cost Supply to deploy.
  • Hold 4 cards. Play one and the next from your 8-card deck slides in.
  • Tap a card, then tap your half of the arena — or drag the card onto the field. Drops snap to legal ground. Esc or drag off the field cancels. Tap a selected card again to deselect.
  • Spells can be aimed anywhere. Troops and pillboxes must land on legal ground.
  • Destroy the enemy Strongpoint to win, or lead in bunker takedowns when time expires.
  • Keys 1–4 select cards. Esc cancels. Same Supply rules for you and the bot.
